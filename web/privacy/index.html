Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy describes how Square Color — the chess training game available on the web and as an Android app — handles your information. The short version: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, sign-ups, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and the game does not use cookies.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best streak (a single number) in your browser's or the app's local storage so it is still there next time you play. This value never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Your current score, accuracy and streak are kept only in memory and are lost when you close the game.

Hosting

The game is hosted on Vercel, and the Android app loads the game from there. Like any web host, Vercel may automatically process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page and protect against abuse. We do not access or use this information.

App permissions

The Android app uses only internet access, which it needs to load the game. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or any other personal data.

Children's privacy

Square Color is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
